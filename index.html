Delete Your Account

We respect your privacy and give you control over your personal information. If you no longer want to use Hisabo – Expense Tracker, you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support email address.
  2. Use the subject: Hisabo Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered with your Hisabo account.
  4. We may contact you to verify your ownership of the account.
  5. After verification, we will process your deletion request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon successful account deletion, we intend to delete:

  • Your registered account information.
  • Your income and expense records stored in your account.
  • Your account and budget information.
  • Your associated cloud-stored financial data.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain information may be retained when required by applicable laws, legal obligations, security requirements or dispute resolution. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable privacy requirements.

Deletion Timeframe

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days. If additional time is required, we will communicate the reason.
